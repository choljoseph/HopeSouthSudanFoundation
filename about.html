About Hope South Sudan Foundation

Serving communities through humanitarian action, development and empowerment.

Who We Are

Hope South Sudan Foundation is a humanitarian and development organization committed to improving lives across South Sudan. We work with communities, government institutions and partners to deliver sustainable solutions in education, healthcare, livelihoods and peacebuilding.

Our Vision

A peaceful and prosperous South Sudan where every person has the opportunity to thrive.

Our Mission

To empower vulnerable communities through inclusive, sustainable development programs.

Our Values

Integrity, compassion, accountability and partnership.
